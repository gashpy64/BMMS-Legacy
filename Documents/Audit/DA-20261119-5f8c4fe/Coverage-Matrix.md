## How to read this matrix

Each cell is "Existence / Verification: path (section)", or "n/a: reason". Existence is Found, Partial or
Missing; Verification is Verified, Unverified or Outdated. A cell that is Missing has no verification (written
"Missing" alone). A WF2a skeleton (headings and CONFIRM lines only) and a doc-writer stub count as Missing.
The CHANGELOG column has Existence only (its Verification is n/a: history, not current behaviour).

Path prefixes: UM = Documents/User-Manuals; SM = Documents/System-Manual; P2P = Documents/Data-Flow/03-Program-to-Program;
WF = Documents/Data-Flow/04-Workflows; CL = Documents/Release-Notes/CHANGELOG.md.

"Dashboard section only" means the manual holds the Dashboard screen and the paragraphs on its cards, and the
sections "What you can and cannot do", "Signing in and your password", "Tasks by goal", "Alerts and mails you receive",
"Common problems" and "Quick reference" are CONFIRM lines (Partial). "Unverified" cells are those whose claims were not
compared with the code in this run. A "Partial / Verified" cell means every claim the manual does write for that area
holds; the missing parts are still gaps.

The n/a reasons come from BMMS/XML/PageAccess.xml (the roles of each page) and BMMS/UserControl/MenuUserControl.ascx
(the menus by role).

Changes of the continuation session (drift check of SM/02, 03, 04, 05, 08, 10 and P2P/09 finished): the P2P/09 cell of
Followup moved to Verified; the P2P/09 cell of Dashboard and alerts moved to Outdated; the SM/05 cells of Reports and of
Dashboard and alerts moved to Outdated (stale statements, see Drift-Register.md). The other cells are unchanged.
